## What the amendment changes

Self-directed equipment improvement, professions, collecting, social life, and a future real player economy are central play, not activities subordinate to completing the prophecy. Traditional chosen classes lead; adaptation must not silently replace a class. History changes future opportunities without erasing past deeds. A friend being able to give another player a desired sword is a future multiplayer acceptance scenario, not an NPC cutscene.

Paid power, offline-loss severity, city-building scope, rare-pet allocation, and exact balance remain unresolved design decisions. Preserving an idea in Git does not ratify every mechanism, nor does it erase the founder's more risk-tolerant ideas in favor of the assistant's recommendations.
