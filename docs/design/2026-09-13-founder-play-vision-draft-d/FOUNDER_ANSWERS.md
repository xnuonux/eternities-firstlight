## 2. Freedom, class, allegiance, and a world that learns the character

good question. i feel like the player should have options to be good or bad, or neutral. one can be a whale p2w that just xonsumes content everywhere across multiple characters. some characters can go the direction of the angels and good, following the ultimate main questline, i think the questline should adapt to the players actions and playstyle. like idk and you can look up games that already do this for reference, but a player may just want to roleplay in a town, beef up his stats,accquire new pets and versions, rare gear, auction, gather materials, farming shit must be doable.people want to no life on ganes like these, they seek mounts for wow, transmogs for gears, customizations, crafting, max out crafting and more. we can dig these primitives from these games. freedom should be vibrant and a player should feel like they are influencing their destiny, with changes in future questlines and npc reactive behavior adapting to players choices. when player goes evil or something, exclusive quests open up in that direction. same for holy,unique quests open up towards whatever the player most is found doing. if player is crafting often, occasionally the game realizes the player, oooh. how about this, and this is all eternities here, fuck. the game learns the player theough time, and the system knows the player, and adjusts the world and gameplay to what is most likely to invoke reaction , interest, continued experience. like in lunari, remembers the user through time. the echo memory, the luna continuous the phenomenological core. something inside the game should monitor every character, sparingly but enough to develop an idea. and the destiny directions should be vast and also deep and adaptive. yes those kinds of roles for players should exist, ruler, merchant, adventurur, hero, savior, all possible titles, and titles system should also be included.
i think the furthest potential from our original idea could be, player goes good, then turns all the way evil, *reaps benefits from one side while losing them from the other* as he goes on, and can either remain one sided or between the spectrum. theres other axis of character development one can go through as well,like mastery of crafts, social status, (tournaments, duels, some kind of leaderboards for various competitions or scores, wealth, power etc), dripping gold radiance and crumbling shadow ash emanence visuals for the characters based on alignment. ultimately, whatever the best axes of this shit are, firstlight should have them available for people. people can live out either sides of the questlines but there is no turnijg back the clock.once a decision is made, the future changes in some way. the same quests and future for someone going pure evil and then coming back to tge light, wont be the same as someone who never went evil at all. that could be a defining class of game.

## 3. Maintenance, loss, private spaces, and consequences

this is a great divergence in traditional mmorpg "the world is shared for all, nothing in it is personalized to individuals". we can break that, and a player can have private sections in the world that change based on the gameplay,and decisions and word events. certain games have housing systems where players can grow flowers and resources that die unless returned to after a while. *while you were away* a pack of raiders stole a piece of gear or wealth. broke some infrastructure. players defenses failed.something like in fallout 4 or whatever game has that settlement system where npc settlers live inside what player makes, and they defend it. idk though, maybe its too much for an mmorpg. this is a hard complication to get by for me. if the player destroys items or gear, thats permaloss. naming weapons could be a good idea as well, if theyre mythic tier, armor as well. player may own a store, and it loses money if he doesnt visit sometimes. pet gets unhappy if not fed, might get weakened from it. visibly dull. armor might get weakened if not maintained, repair system should exist too. death should act like wow and dull equipment. expand on that area of exploration.

## 4. Player society, guild property, raids, and social channels

this question unlocks a lot. perhaps players should be able to build their own cities and towns, collaborately. conflict of interests becomes problematic. these maybe should already exist and players can simply be named authorities in them, the building thing might be too much, minecraft flavor might in fact push this beyond what it can sensibly maintain. unless you ideate a solution, idk. maybe guilds can have bases, sort of like towns. publicly accessible? visitable? locked? in wow an alliance has an alliance chest which can be accessed with ranked approval from members. the answer could lie in that functionality. but when it comes to hostile looting from other players, yes. but i think that part should be studied and taken from the gatcha rts games like clash of kings, clash of clans, and such where other kings choose to send troops to attack and burn cities and loot. raises their kill rankings and lowers power levels. i dont know how to quantify this mechanic into a freebase form where we could mold into a personal character feature that can be interacted with by other players, hostile or ally. this might turn the game into an rts if not bounded, and the complexity shouldnt reach ridiculous capacity. if we could simplify these features while retainining their glamour and allures. towns should be filled with pkayers just socializing and shit, hanging out with their toons , showing off gear, having that classic wow chat experience, chat bubbles above their heads, in the chat box. dms, guild chat, world chat, local chat, yell.

## 5. The remembered experience

the story should be as impactful as the lord of the rings and hobbit stories were. like harry potter, like pirates of the carribean, i want the game to be memorable in the way that it teaches the player a deep lesson they can use for real life. something that grows their spirit. good or bad, good to show mercy and love.bad would show examples of the evil that exists and could leave the player knowing what the result may look like. inwant people to walk away from this game with an experience they can keep with them long after they play it. whatever psychological magic that goes on in peopels minds that brings out these kinds of permanent legacy retentions, i want to explore those, the empathic ones, the sad ones, the ones that leave an impact inside someone, the music was that good... the art and graphics were so immersive, the fighting felt so real, the human interactions were unforgettable. the joys of playing and spending tine upgrading and evolving, making friends, the conflicts, the anger, the struggle to win, pain. the best wins are the ones you keep your pain with. it cost that much pain and effort to get that reward. thats the thesis.
