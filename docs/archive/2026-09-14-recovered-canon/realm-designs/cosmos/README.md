## Design status

`Cosmos — The Unwritten Meridian` is a completed **design package, not a playable build**. Its package receipt records 10 provinces, 40 story threads, 10 event families, 6 campaign movements, 24 prototype planning points, 32 abstract links, 4 planned encounters and 3 local resolutions. The package's own checks reported 51 design-consistency passes and 30 checker tests; those are documentation/authoring checks, not gameplay or performance evidence.

The design preserves the existing Firstlight cosmology and the retained Atlas/Pixel/Echo/Luna responsibilities. It does not move the Regent's Earth confrontation, complete the Answering, replace the Empyrean/Abyss/Atlantis designs, create a new economy, or connect a live Luna service.

## Gameplay-source boundary

The design inspected PR #13, `gameplay/outing-readability`, at `bdad75b70b7762f6ef89fe0982ebc07cd4ddef0c`. That is an observed reference, not an instruction to replace newer work.
